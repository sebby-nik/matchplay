The captain’s handbook

A perfect twelve.

  1. Draft on instinct. Pick one of three golfers, twelve times. Each card represents a particular year. Ratings stay hidden until your team is complete.
  2. Trust your knowledge. You have two rerolls to replace an offer. Picks are permanent, and each golfer appears just once.
  3. Make your calls. See both teams’ ratings, then tap two of your players to swap their positions against the fixed opposition.
  4. Let the session unfold. Lock your lineup and reveal the matches. A win earns 1 point, a half earns ½. More than 6 wins the session. Twelve wins is the perfect sweep.

Higher-rated golfers are more likely to win, but upsets and halves happen. Ratings reflect recorded singles matchplay, rather than overall career achievements. Cards use the final recorded rating of their year, after at least ten matches.

Everyone gets the same daily offers, opponents and simulation randomness. Your choices still matter. The daily resets at midnight UTC; progress is saved in this browser. Practice is unlimited and has its own personal best.
